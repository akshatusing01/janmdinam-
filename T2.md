# 2. CONTEXT-DRIFT AUDIT — KEY FINDING

The main problem was **not that ChatGPT forgot Purvi's likes**.

The more important failure was that later ChatGPT responses gradually blurred three different categories:

- confirmed decisions,
- source-derived facts/observations,
- creative proposals.

Creative ideas were sometimes promoted into architecture or "locked" design decisions without an explicit confirmation checkpoint.

### Important boundary identified

The Purvi preference material itself was still handled relatively correctly. It explicitly distinguished **Purvi's personal preferences** from the project's design palette and warned against forcing favourites into the experience merely because they existed.

The drift became much clearer later, especially when:

- Memory Graph was treated as more architectural than its original "creative concept" status warranted.
- Purvi DNA was treated as an architectural layer despite originating as a creative concept.
- Specific simulators (e.g. Rapido/BMHRC) risked becoming assumed features instead of candidate treatments.
- Phase 5.1 declared a visual design system "locked" even though earlier project material had explicitly said final UI, navigation, technology architecture, Vault mechanics, and journey sequence were not yet locked.
- The historical 10-section birthday structure later appeared as implementation architecture even though it had previously been described as a planned/historical structure rather than immutable final architecture.

### Core diagnosis

> The project was not lost; **the boundary between "we decided this" and "ChatGPT suggested this" was lost.**
