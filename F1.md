- Keep F1 concise but loss-aware: preserve decisions and important reasoning, not every conversational sentence.
- Before any new project work, read F1 and reconcile the requested change against it.

- Never treat model memory, a later AI suggestion, current code, or a generated design as truth merely because it exists.
- F1 is the continuity source; source files/history remain the evidence source.

## 1. DECISION / EVIDENCE STATUS
- CONFIRMED = explicitly decided/approved by Akshat.
- SOURCE-DERIVED = factual material supplied in project sources; preserve faithfully.
- PROPOSED = idea suggested during design; not a requirement until approved.
- PENDING = requires explicit confirmation before implementation.
- LEGACY = exists in earlier AI-generated architecture/code but is not automatically approved.
- REJECTED = explicitly rejected; do not reintroduce.
- Never convert PROPOSED/LEGACY/PENDING into CONFIRMED silently.
- Never invent historical facts, dates, conversations, preferences, or media.
- If a source and a later AI statement conflict, preserve the source-supported fact and flag the conflict rather than silently reconciling it.
